How Milestone-Based Funding Works

EduFund replaces blind trust with on-chain cryptographic accountability.

1

Milestone Pledges

Creators lock clear verifiable phases with allocated amounts. Backers pledge ETH into the smart contract escrow.

2

Backer Consensus

When creator delivers a milestone, backers vote with their pledged weight. Upon reaching >50% consensus, the creator unlocks that tranche.

3

100% Refund Guarantee

If a campaign fails to hit its overall funding goal before the deadline, contributors can instantly claim back 100% of their deposited ETH.

Connecting MetaMask

Resolve browser extension detection

1 Recommended: Open via Local Server (HTTP)

Chrome and Brave disable all browser extensions on local file:// paths for security. To let MetaMask connect, run the built-in server:

node server.js

Then open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.

2 Or: Allow File Access in Extension Settings

If you want to keep viewing directly from your disk:

  1. Open chrome://extensions in your browser.
  2. Click Details under MetaMask.
  3. Toggle ON the switch for "Allow access to file URLs".
  4. Refresh this page.
3 Unlock MetaMask & Reload

Click the MetaMask Fox icon in your browser toolbar, unlock it with your password, and reload the tab.
